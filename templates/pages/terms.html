Terms of Service

Rules and operational parameters governing participation on BallotBridge.

1. One Person, One Vote

Participants agree not to create duplicate accounts or attempt to cast more than one ballot in any single election. Violations are automatically detected and rejected by database transaction locks.

2. Irreversibility of Ballots

Once a voter submits and confirms their ballot selection, the vote is immediately anonymized and sealed into the election vault. Ballots cannot be modified, withdrawn, or re-voted.

3. Candidate Conduct & Fair Presentation

Candidates in simulated elections are displayed in neutral ballot order. The platform does not endorse, rank, or weight any candidate over another.

4. Demonstration Platform Status

BallotBridge is a simulated portfolio system. It is not licensed for binding governmental, statutory, or legally binding parliamentary elections.
